2 · Pages

There is no sidebar any more: the inputs sit inside each page. Inputs, displays, data and functions.

Home

streamlit/home_page.py

Mandate, how to use the app, data coverage and roadmap.

Inputs

  • None (link button “Start with Investor Preferences”)

Displays

  • Banner image assets/leman_banner.png
  • 4 metrics: equity universe, eligible at start, ESG coverage, bond & commodity indices
  • Bar chart: stocks per zone (all constituents vs eligible)
  • Table: coverage per zone (stocks, eligible, ESG coverage, median ESG)
  • Table: benchmarks and status · list of non-equity sleeves
  • Roadmap badges out of date

Data & functions

  • load_universe → esg_universe.parquet
  • load_series_names → prices_bonds / commodities / benchmarks
  • coverage_by_zone, universe_totals, benchmark_status

Investor Preferences

streamlit/investor_preferences.py

Step 1: the investor's questionnaire; runs the ESG screen.

Inputs

  • Region pills: US, Eurozone, Pacific, EM EMEA
  • Toggle “All industries” + multiselect of 11 ICB industries (with stock counts)
  • Select slider “ESG ambition”: None / Low / Medium / High / Very high (0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 0.9 percentile)
  • Toggle “Eligible at portfolio start only (2005-01-01)”
  • Buttons: Screen universe, Reset

Displays

  • “Your selection” card: stocks in scope, eligible, summary of answers, validation errors
  • Result metrics: in scope → eligible → screened candidates → ESG-selected
  • Warnings when the ESG cut was relaxed · table of requested vs effective cut per zone

Data & functions

  • validate_preferences, industry_counts, missing_industries_by_zone
  • cached_screening → run_screening → core.esg.investor_selection (best-in-class within zone × industry, relaxed by 0.05 if fewer than 10 stocks)
  • Writes session_state["screening_result"]

Universe Explorer

streamlit/universe_explorer.py

Step 2: inspect the screened stocks before they are weighted.

Inputs

  • Text search “Search for a company”
  • Multiselect “Filter by region”
  • Download button (CSV)

Displays

  • 4 metrics: in scope, eligible, selected, ESG ambition
  • Bar charts: stocks by region and by industry
  • ESG metrics (mean, median, coverage) + histogram of ESG scores
  • Expander: screening warnings + effective ESG cut
  • Table of selected companies (company, region, country, industry, sector, ESG, ISIN)

Data & functions

  • Reads only session_state["screening_result"]; without it, shows “apply your preferences first”

ERC Portfolio

streamlit/portfolio.py

Step 3: two-level ERC on the screened universe at the latest monthly rebalancing date, plus the historical backtest.

Inputs

  • Radio: “Equal risk contribution (ERC)” vs “ESG-tilted risk budgets”
  • Selectbox “Sleeve” (Risk contributions tab)
  • Multiselect “Sleeves” + text search + CSV download (Holdings tab)
  • Universe from the Preferences page; default = all regions, Medium ESG

Displays

  • 4 metrics: portfolio volatility, assets held, equity share, risk-contribution check
  • Allocation: donut by sleeve, sleeve table (weight, risk contribution, volatility), top-15 positions
  • Risk contributions: weight vs risk contribution by sleeve (target 1/N) and inside a sleeve
  • ERC vs Equal Weight: volatility table EW / ERC / Min-Variance + risk-contribution bars
  • Holdings: per-asset weight, share of risk, volatility, ESG
  • Historical backtest: growth of 100 (log scale), drawdowns, metrics table for ERC, ESG, 60/40, 1/N

Data & functions

  • read_processed("returns_daily", …) per zone + bonds + commodities
  • estimation_window → build_erc_portfolio (covariance_matrix, risk_budget_weights, risk_contributions, _min_variance)
  • reference_backtest → portfolio_returns.parquet, performance_metrics.csv; drawdown
  • crisis_performance.csv and sleeve_weights_erc are loaded but not shown
